CEB Progressive Blocks Hit Hard Above 300 kWh

From 1 May 2026, CEB domestic tariffs (110/120/140) rise in 12 blocks. Below 300 kWh is relatively cheap. From 301-500 kWh you pay Rs 12.03/kWh. Above that it climbs to Rs 13.06/kWh. Households using 400-800+ kWh/month feel the pain every month.

Solar alone helps daytime. Hybrid with battery (Deye inverter + Pylontech) shifts solar power to evening peaks (18-21h) when CEB demand is highest and your consumption is costly.
