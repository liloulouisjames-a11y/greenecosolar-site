Headline offering

Agrivoltaics

Raised solar structures let land keep producing food while it also produces electricity.

Same land, two harvests

Panels sit high enough for crops, workers and equipment to move underneath. The field stays a field.

Shade where it helps

Partial shade can reduce heat stress and water loss for some crops. Suitability depends on the crop and site, and we assess this with you.

Energy for the farm

Power for pumps, cold rooms and sheds, generated where it is used.

Small farmers and planters

We can help planters and small farmers look at how agrivoltaics fits with schemes such as those relating to the Small Farmers Welfare Fund (SFWF) and other applicable programmes. Eligibility and terms are set by the relevant authorities; we help you prepare the technical side and check the current conditions with you before you commit.

FAQ

What is agrivoltaics?

Solar panels mounted above farmland so crops and power share the same land.

Will you help with the CEB grant?

Yes. We help prepare and submit the paperwork and explain the current CEB conditions.

Can I add a battery later?

Usually yes, if the system is planned for it. We design with that in mind.

Do you install EV chargers?

Yes, for homes and businesses, with or without solar.

Where do you work?

Across Mauritius, from our base in Trou-aux-Biches.
