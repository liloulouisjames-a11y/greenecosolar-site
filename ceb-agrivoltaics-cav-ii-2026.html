CEB Agrivoltaics (CAV) II Scheme 2026: Double Your Land Income in Mauritius

Registered planters and farmers can now apply under the revised CEB Agrivoltaics (CAV) II Scheme (July 2026) for hybrid solar PV + battery projects up to 4 MWac. Same land produces crops and electricity.

Who qualifies

  • Registered planters and farmers (individual or via cooperative)
  • Hybrid systems (solar + battery storage) required
  • Multiple projects per developer possible
  • Total scheme capacity limited — apply early

Why Deye + Pylontech hybrid

Deye hybrid inverters manage solar, battery, and grid seamlessly. Pylontech LiFePO4 batteries deliver long cycle life for evening farm loads and backup. Systems are engineered for tropical conditions and CEB grid code.
