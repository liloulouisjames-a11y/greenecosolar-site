How Dual Income Works Under CAV II

  • Registered planters/farmers (or cooperative) only.
  • Raised structures keep full farming access underneath.
  • Hybrid required: solar PV + battery (minimum 2 hours at half AC output).
  • Electricity sold to CEB or used on-farm; crops continue.
