Cyclone-Resistant Solar Mounting in Mauritius: Protect Your System from the Start

Mauritius sits in the cyclone belt. Historical storms have reached Category 5 intensity with sustained winds over 250 km/h and gusts higher. A solar array is a large sail. Cheap or under-engineered mounts become projectiles, destroy roofs, and wipe out your investment in panels, Deye hybrid inverters and Pylontech batteries.

First-Principles Wind Load Design

  • Calculate design wind speed from local codes and historical data for your site (coastal vs inland differs).
  • Use structural-grade aluminium rails and stainless fasteners rated for the load. No thin imported kits that deform at 150 km/h.
  • Prefer penetrating mounts into structural members with proper flashing over pure ballast on flat roofs where uplift is high.
  • Module clamps, mid- and end-clamps, and rail spacing engineered so panels stay put. We never skimp on the hardware that holds the array.
  • Roof integrity first: we survey structure, waterproofing and load capacity before any install.
