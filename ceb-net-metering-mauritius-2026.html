CEB Net Metering Mauritius 2026: Cut Your Electricity Bill with the Household Solar Scheme

The CEB Household Rooftop Solar Photovoltaic (PV) Scheme 2026 gives domestic customers a clear path to lower bills. Systems export up to 10 kWac. Government grant covers 25% of system cost up to Rs 75,000 via Development Bank of Mauritius. Application fee Rs 2,000.

Net Metering vs Gross Metering

  • Gross metering: Every kWh produced is credited at Rs 4.83.
  • Net metering (Tariff 150A_NM): Production first offsets your consumption. Surplus exported credited at Rs 3.00/kWh. Solidarity contribution on offset energy above thresholds.
  • Agreement runs 20 years.
  • Battery optional below 3.5 kWac; required above that for evening coverage under current rules.

Net metering usually saves more when daytime loads (AC, pool pumps, water heating) are high because offsetting a high-tariff unit beats the fixed Rs 4.83 credit. Gross is simpler if you are rarely home during the day.
